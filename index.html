Our Heritage

From a Humble Beginning to a Senior Secondary Beacon

First Step Senior Secondary School opened its doors in 1992 with an enduring vision: to deliver affordable, high-caliber, values-driven education that honors each child's innate potential.

Over the past 34 years, our campus has expanded into a full Nursery through Class XII CBSE institution. We keep our student-teacher ratios disciplined so learners are mentored with patience and individual attention.

"विद्या प्रथमो चरण:"
"Knowledge is the first and most fundamental step toward self-discovery, character, and lifelong leadership."

Our Vision

To cultivate an inclusive and inspiring learning ecosystem where each child discovers their unique talents, strives for academic excellence, and matures into an ethical, responsible citizen.

Our Mission

To blend academic excellence with creative problem-solving, modern digital literacy, and moral integrity, ensuring every learner receives personal attention to excel in board examinations and life.

Enroll at First Step School

Step-by-Step Roadmap

How to Apply

1

Submit Online Enquiry

Complete the form alongside or visit the school office on Khapa Bhat Road to register your interest.

2

Campus Visit & Interaction

Meet our faculty counselors and take a guided tour of our smart classrooms, computer lab, and grounds.

3

Document Verification

Submit required records (Birth certificate, Transfer Certificate, Aadhaar card copies, and passport photos).

4

Seat Confirmation

Complete formal enrolment and fee formalities to confirm your child's seat.

Required Documents Checklist

  • Original Birth Certificate (Pre-Primary & Class 1)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school (Class 2+)
  • Aadhaar Card copies of Student and Parents
  • Previous Class Academic Marksheet / Report Card
  • 4 Recent Passport Size Photographs of Student
